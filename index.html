Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to book in advance?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking ahead guarantees your preferred time and stylist, especially on weekends.

Do you offer bridal trials?

Yes. Our bridal packages include a free makeup trial so you can finalise your look before the wedding.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, UPI and all major cards.

Are your products safe for sensitive skin?

We use trusted branded products and can suggest gentle, ammonia-free options. Please tell us about any allergies before your service.
